If a picture is worth a 1000 words, then this picture speaks volumes about me.

I have no excuse not to have published a web site until recently. I've been in IT 30+ years. Just like the picture, I like to observe. I also learn best by **doing**. That's what this site is all about.

Topics will vary greatly but will center around homelabs and using enterprise software, processes, practices and methodologies for personal IT. You can call it an "opinionated way" of doing things. I even thought about trying to coin the term **PITA**, for "Personal IT Architecture". Unfortunately that acronym already has a pejorative meaning (you'll have to Google it to find out what that is).

One goal of mine is to **not** make "personal IT"... a PITA.

While I have never been one to shy away from complexity, I do plan to tackle some complex topics, probably from the start, and hopefully explain them in a less complex way.

So please, check back often. I hope the content will be of value to you.
